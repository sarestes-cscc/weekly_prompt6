Question:

In Chapter 13, the rocket ship fires bullets to destroy the alien 
spaceships. This is accomplished by using the sprite utility groupcollide(). 
Search the pygame documentation or other resources on the web and discuss 
what this method does.

Answer:

The groupcollide() method is similar to the spritecollide() method in that it 
compares two things and wether they touch (collide) and what happens if they 
do collide, the difference is groupcollide() can compare groups and whether 
a sprite in a group collides with a separate sprite or sprite group. The first 
two variables in the parentheses are your two group(s)/sprite. There are other 
functions that you can use with the Group element, to edit the group by adding, 
subtracting, completely emptying, etc. Once you have the Group defined then you 
can test whether it collides using groupcollide().
